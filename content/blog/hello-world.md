## Hello, World! 🌿

Welcome to **thegoldenpothos** blog! I'm Keith, and this is where I'll be sharing my thoughts on software engineering, plants, coffee, and photography.

### What to Expect

Here's a taste of what I plan to write about:

- **Software Engineering** — Lessons learned building full-stack applications, working with React, ASP.NET, and cloud services.
- **Plant Care** — Tips for keeping your pothos (and other houseplants) thriving.
- **Coffee** — Brew methods, bean reviews, and café explorations.
- **Photography** — Behind the lens and my favorite shots.

### Why a Blog?

I've always believed that **writing clarifies thinking**. This blog is as much for me as it is for you — a way to document my journey, solidify what I learn, and hopefully help others along the way.

Stay tuned for more!
