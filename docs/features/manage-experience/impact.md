**Risks**

- A move rewrites the positions of every entry whose index changed (a handful of documents); two tabs moving at once can leave two entries at the same position (likelihood very low, cost low: ties sort by id, and the next move renumbers). Carried by D-09 and the tie rule of `sortEntries` (E2E-04).
- A timed-out or slow save can land after the owner has seen "could not be saved" (likelihood low, cost low: last save wins, the owner re-saves). Carried by D-12, OQ-2.
- The Firestore SDK logs a `console.error` line when the rules refuse a write; no row asserts the console is clean on the panel. Cleared: the new files never log (E2E-28 checks `/console\./`).
- A hand-written `experience` document (a field missing or of the wrong type, no `position`) must not crash the list (likelihood low). Carried by TS-02, TS-03 and E2E-03, E2E-04.
- The generic `ListEditor` takes render and store props that the next three editors depend on; a vague contract here is repeated three times (likelihood medium, cost medium). Carried by TS-06, TS-07 and D-24: Step 2 gives the exact props, and E2E-29 pins that the shared wording lives only in the shared files.

**Cleared**

- `firestore.rules` already allows public read and owner-only write on `experience/{id}`: no rules change, no deploy or emulator change (`firestore.rules:21-24`).
- Public pages stay Firebase-free: nothing under `src/app/components/*.tsx`, `src/app/page.tsx` or the blog imports the new modules (`git grep -n "components/admin\|lib/firebase\|lib/admin" -- src` finds only admin files and the panel route); E2E-31 asserts it.
- Static export: the editor is a client component whose Firestore calls happen inside effects and handlers; `getFirebaseDb()` is never called at import or render (it throws when `window` is undefined, `client.ts`).
- No new dependency: `firebase/firestore` is already installed and used by `images.ts` and `profile.ts`.
- No picture is shown, so no `next/image` and no `<img>` lint concern.
- `AdminPanel.tsx`'s section ids are unchanged, so the earlier specs' seven-item menu assertions hold.
