**Risks**

- **The earlier unnamed-collection row and the menu, notice and `deploy:rules` expectations all change.** Confirmed by the walks above. Likelihood certain, cost low. Carried: D-24; Step 1 repoints three rows and Step 3 two more, each located by title; every other row of both specs must still pass (Verify runs both whole files).
- **Rules are project-wide, and the storage rules are deployed with the Firestore rules** from the live job only. Same reasoning as `admin-owner-only-access` D-14; `deploy-staging` is untouched (AC-14).
- **The live bucket does not exist yet and the deploy account may lack a role.** `firebase deploy --only storage` reads the default bucket and fails until the owner has created it and granted Rules Admin. Likelihood high until then, cost low (the step after hosting fails; the site has deployed). Carried: OQ-1.
- **Emulator versus production rules engine.** The constructs used are the documented common subset; nothing here ran against the live bucket. Carried: OQ-2.
- **Global emulator state shared across spec files.** Storage objects and the global rules are not per spec. Likelihood low in the pipeline (one spec per command), medium for the full suite. Carried: D-26, OQ-3, `adminClear()` at the start of every screen row.
- **A guessable download address would expose an image.** The generated UUID name plus the download token, plus the refused `list`, are the protection; Assumption 5 asks nothing stronger. Carried: BR-08f, E2E-08.
- **A hung upload on a dropped connection.** The library's default retry limit is ten minutes. Carried: D-16, E2E-42.
- **Uploading to a stale Storage emulator on a developer machine.** Carried: Step 1 Stop-if.
- **Bucket name guess.** `storageBucket` is the console's default pattern, not read from the console. Carried: D-20, OQ-1.
