**Risks**

- **R-1 The real SQL and library are never run by automation** (decomposition Open finding 1). Proven on PostgreSQL itself by SF-2; not proven through Supabase's pooler. Likelihood low, cost one SIT round. Carried as Decision D-16, E2E-31 (manual) and Step 2's Stop-if.
- **R-2 Tab divergence (assumption 15).** Two tabs adding or moving at once can leave two entries at the same position; they then list in insertion order. Likelihood low (one owner), cost a manual re-move. Accepted, Decision D-12; E2E-25 shows last save wins.
- **R-3 An earlier spec is edited by a later feature.** Each later list slice finds "Blog posts" notices in three earlier specs until the blog slice. Likelihood certain for the blog slice, cost one line. Recorded in D-11 for the later writers.
- **R-4 A paused Supabase project** shows "The entries could not be loaded." (accepted in intent §4). No keep-awake job.
