**Risks**

- **R-1** (confirmed, certain, low cost): `project.tsx` is listed as an unchanged public source and as a file that must still hold old-backend text in `e2e/retire-firebase.spec.ts` and `e2e/hosting-and-backend-migration-acceptance.spec.ts`, and five earlier tests expect the Projects section on a blank site (Impact table above). They fail after Step 1 until Step 2 amends them. Carried: TS-06, Step 2.
- **R-2** (unproven, low likelihood, medium cost): if the page's projects read fails during a regeneration while Publish's profile read succeeded, the framework's behaviour (keep the old copy, or fail the visit) is unproven here. The same open item exists for the profile and Experience. Both reads go to one database through one client. Carried: Open Question OQ-1 and D-04; `site-survives-database-outage` proves the outage case for every section.
- **R-3** (confirmed): `src/app/page.tsx` must keep exactly one occurrence of the word `revalidate` (the profile feature's E2E-21 counts it). Carried: TS-03 and Step 1's Change.
- **R-4** (confirmed, certain, low cost): the two sibling slices (tech stack, certifications) edit the same lines of `src/app/page.tsx`, `src/app/components/header.tsx`, `CLAUDE.md`, `Reference.md` and the earlier specs' lists. Everything here is located by searching and edits only the Projects part. Carried: Steps 1 to 3 (each Change names what to search) and the note in this document's header.
- **R-5** (confirmed, low): `e2e/hosting-and-backend-migration-acceptance.spec.ts` E2E-ACC-30 and `e2e/owner-manages-projects.spec.ts` save projects and expect `/` to stay byte-identical. That stays true because saving does not renew the page; re-run in Step 2.
- **R-6** (cleared as a risk, kept as a note): the seven written-in projects are lost from the code. The pull request must name the commit of Step 1 so the owner can re-enter them by hand (story Assumption 8, intent §8). Carried: D-13.
- **R-7** (confirmed, low): the blog pages keep a "Projects" menu link that points at `/#projects` even when the section is hidden, because the blog layout passes no props (same as the profile slice's "About" link, its D-08). Carried: D-10, OQ-2.
